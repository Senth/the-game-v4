# Personas

## Player

A player uses a phone with a group during an evening puzzle hunt. Other teams are racing them.
They read quest content together, try answers and decide whether a hint is worth its penalty.
They glance at time and rank, then return to the puzzle. More than one phone may share the same team login.

Keep the answer dock reachable at every scroll position on a 390 px screen.
Time and pace stay visible without taking attention from the quest. Hint penalties are readable before revealing.
Teammate progress arrives live, without reloading or repeatedly polling a healthy stream.
The player should never see authoring notes, internal titles, answers or unrevealed hint text.

## Game master

A game master authors at a desk when possible. At the venue they use a phone to fix a quest,
add hints or build the whole game on-site. Phone admin is not a reduced set of controls.

Desktop uses a season tree, quest form and phone-frame player preview. Phone uses Quests, Teams and Settings
tabs, with an Edit/Preview toggle in the quest editor. Both save fields without a Save bar.
During play the game master checks completion and hint reveals, opens a team to see its history and current quest,
and adjusts the end time. Structural changes show how many teams will be affected before confirmation.
